A little more about me

A human perspective.
A product mind.

I’m Alexandra Mena Sansano. I work where product direction, human behaviour and delivery have to meet.

I trained in audiovisual communication and photography before moving into digital product design. Narrative, context and visual sensitivity still shape how I work: I pay attention to how people interpret a system before asking them to use it.

I can own decisions as a senior individual contributor or direct a multidisciplinary team. In both cases, I stay close enough to protect the product’s logic and broad enough to connect the business and technical realities.

On AI

The interesting question is
what changes for the person.

I have led LLM-based products and AI-assisted decision tools, and used AI to build interactive prototypes. I’m interested in the task, the evidence and the room left for human judgement. A convincing prototype still has to prove itself in context.

The role of AI differs between projects. In Coca-Cola and CaixaBank, it is part of the product proposition. In Zip Water, it helped the design and prototyping process. I keep that distinction visible.
