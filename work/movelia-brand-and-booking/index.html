01 / The brief

What needed
to change.

The brand and website had become dated. Movelia needed a more contemporary, human expression that could carry across its digital assets and the journey that mattered most: finding and booking travel.

02 / My responsibility

The work
I owned.

I led the project end to end and the direct client relationship, with three product designers, one visual lead and two narrative and UX writing specialists. I shaped the narrative behind the identity and guided the decisions connecting it to the digital product.

03 / Decisions

Where I shaped
the direction.

01

Define the narrative first.

Establish the principles that would give the new logo, colour system and digital experience a shared direction.

02

Connect expression and behaviour.

Carry the identity into information architecture, navigation and the reservation journey, making the product part of the brand transformation.

03

Keep disciplines moving together.

Align narrative, visual design, product design and the client around the final decisions and the work needed for implementation.

04 / Delivery

What made
it tangible.

  • Strategic narrative and identity direction
  • Logo and brand guidelines
  • Website architecture and reservation experience
  • Propagated screens, documentation and technical handoff
05 / Outcome

The result,
with its boundaries.

The identity and digital product package were delivered for implementation. The project was still in implementation when documented in September 2026.

What is not established

Launch, conversion, usability and adoption results are not confirmed. Unreleased identity assets and interfaces are not included in this public narrative.
