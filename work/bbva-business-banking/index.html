01 / The brief

What needed
to change.

Architecture, content and interaction had evolved differently across Spain, Portugal, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Uruguay. A shared experience had to respect local regulation, technical dependencies and the way each market operated.

02 / My responsibility

The work
I owned.

I led the global platform from definition to implementation, with three to four designers and two UI developers. Aligning more than 72 stakeholders was part of the design work. I later led GEMA and the operational delivery of Financial Health, with up to five projects running in parallel.

03 / Decisions

Where I shaped
the direction.

01

Find the common model.

Define the information architecture, content hierarchy and navigation that could give the platform a coherent structure across markets.

02

Make local differences explicit.

Account for legal and technical requirements without allowing every exception to become a separate product. Work within BBVA’s established design system and implementation frameworks.

03

Carry the model into mobile.

For GEMA, prioritise the financial tasks relevant to sole traders and small businesses while accommodating different technology stacks and legacy systems.

04

Maintain a direction across teams.

In Financial Health, review key functionality, defend decisions with business, product and technology, and guide the current design system through a continuing delivery roadmap.

04 / Delivery

What made
it tangible.

  • Navigable models with market-specific scenarios
  • Screen-level documentation and technical handoff
  • Responsive interaction patterns
  • Programme governance and design-system application
05 / Outcome

The result,
with its boundaries.

The global platform, GEMA and Financial Health functionality were implemented and remain operational, as documented in September 2026. Across the wider BBVA relationship, I shared account-development responsibility with the account director; the account grew by almost 30% during that period.

What is not established

The growth figure is a shared account result. Usage, conversion and efficiency metrics are not available. The six-market scope belongs to the global platform; GEMA’s exact geographic scope is not confirmed.
