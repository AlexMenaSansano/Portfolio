A bank manager’s working day. A two-week brief.

The briefMy responsibilityDecisionsDeliveryOutcome
01 / The brief

What needed
to change.

The tool needed to help branch managers prepare conversations, understand a client’s financial context and compare possible scenarios. The concept was also a proof point for a proposed 60-FTE engagement.

02 / My responsibility

The work
I owned.

I carried out focused qualitative research, worked with the Insights team and banking specialists, and used Bank of Spain sector data to ground the concept. I owned the product decisions and interaction model; a specialist visual team supported the final visual expression.

03 / Decisions

Where I shaped
the direction.

01

Work from the manager’s needs.

Translate research and banking knowledge into a journey for preparation, context and next actions, with a scope that could be delivered within the sprint.

02

Give AI a useful role.

Bring AI into preparation and scenario comparison, keeping factual information distinct from hypothetical generated scenarios.

03

Make the concept coherent end to end.

Define the navigation and interaction across ten screens, supported by a reusable visual system, accessibility guidance and a navigable prototype.

04 / Delivery

What made
it tangible.

  • Ten connected screens and a navigable prototype
  • Navigation and interaction model
  • UI kit and design system
  • Accessibility guide and documentation
05 / Outcome

The result,
with its boundaries.

The package was delivered in less than two weeks. The project contributed to securing 40 of the 60 planned FTE for VML The Cocktail and opened the possibility of a design account with CaixaBank.

What is not established

The FTE figure is a commercial team outcome, not a personal team size. Production deployment, user validation, adoption and operational gains are not confirmed.
