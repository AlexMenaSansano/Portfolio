01 / The brief

What needed
to change.

The screen had to work with physical controls, different product formats and short interactions. Wet hands and limited attention changed the assumptions that would normally shape an app. Levels of digital confidence were a design hypothesis to test.

02 / My responsibility

The work
I owned.

I worked with industrial design and two key stakeholders to understand functions and restrictions, then developed five digital concepts independently. The work was conducted in English. My responsibility was the digital interaction, not the leadership of the industrial design team.

03 / Decisions

Where I shaped
the direction.

01

Begin with the physical context.

Consider what the person is doing in the kitchen, how the tap behaves and the attention available for a screen interaction.

02

Connect controls and feedback.

Define a coherent relationship between physical input, screen states and the product’s response within the hardware constraints.

03

Make alternatives testable.

Build five distinct concepts and animated interactive prototypes, using AI throughout conceptualisation and prototyping to make the proposals tangible for comparison.

04 / Delivery

What made
it tangible.

  • Five digital interaction concepts
  • Five interface designs
  • Five animated interactive prototypes
  • Simulations of the tap’s physical controls
05 / Outcome

The result,
with its boundaries.

The concepts and prototypes were delivered for selection and validation. The product line was planned for 2027 when the work was documented in September 2026.

What is not established

The final concept, workshop findings and launch are not confirmed. AI supported the design process; an AI capability in the final tap is not claimed.
