The briefMy responsibilityDecisionsDeliveryOutcome
01 / The brief

What needed
to change.

Teams moved between emails, documents and multiple tools to develop campaigns, find earlier work and reconnect with people or agencies. The product opportunity was to make that knowledge easier to reach through a common experience.

02 / My responsibility

The work
I owned.

I led design direction and day-to-day product decisions, collaborating daily with at least five technology specialists. Up to seven designers contributed at different stages. My manager and I shared stakeholder conversations; decisions of wider scope were reviewed by project leadership.

03 / Decisions

Where I shaped
the direction.

01

Start with the marketing task.

Frame the experience around finding precedents, developing ideas and reaching relevant collaborators. Make the purpose of the AI clear in the product model.

02

Define a credible MVP.

Shape one interaction model for four programmes, balancing the future experience with what design and technology could demonstrate together.

03

Keep design and technology close.

Use daily collaboration to resolve feasibility questions and bring executive product decisions to the right level of review.

04 / Delivery

What made
it tangible.

  • An internal LLM-based MVP
  • A unified interaction model for four programmes
  • A proposed future-state experience
  • Design and technology alignment throughout
05 / Outcome

The result,
with its boundaries.

An internal MVP and future-state experience were completed. The work demonstrated a common entry point to four programmes and a product direction for knowledge access.

What is not established

The project was documented as work in progress in September 2026. Production deployment, adoption and efficiency gains are not confirmed. The degree of functional integration is not independently established here.
